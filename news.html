About NiCER

NiCER is a non-profit network that connects researchers, universities, industry, government and funders, turning research into innovation, enterprise and social impact across Africa.

NiCER Network NPC is the social initiative of Khoro and is registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC).
