NiCER Academy

Accessible, industry-led and accredited learning on research commercialisation, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Objective 2Programme 6.2
Take the psychometric test

Objective 2: To build innovation capability

The NiCER Academy develops capability across the innovation ecosystem. It offers accessible courses and workshops on practical concepts relating to the ICE Nexus and will run as a standalone learning platform at academy.nicer.org.

Why it matters

Most research outputs are not commercialised, partly because researchers, students and professionals have never been taught how. Capability is the foundation for every other objective.

What we do

We offer workshops and courses, many industry-led and some accredited for CPD points or by SETAs, on research commercialisation, IP management, commercialisation roadmaps, innovation and entrepreneurship. We deliver them with industry, professional bodies and academic institutions.

Intended outcomes

  • Commercialisation-ready postgraduate students and graduates.
  • Professionals and officials who understand innovation concepts.
  • Companies able to meet skills development targets through relevant, accredited learning.

NiCER Academy

Accessible, industry-led courses and workshops on the ICE Nexus, including CPD-accredited and SETA-accredited learning.

Visit academy.nicer.org

Who the Academy is for

  • Postgraduate students and early-career researchers
  • Graduates preparing for the world of work
  • Engineers and other professionals who need continuing professional development
  • Government officials and public servants
  • Company employees, through their employers’ skills development programmes
  • Entrepreneurs and university officials who support student entrepreneurs

Flagship course: research commercialisation for postgraduate students

Postgraduate students are typically focused on finishing, publishing and graduating. This course trains them to also think about how their research could be commercialised. Topics include:

  • The ICE Nexus and the journey from research to market
  • Intellectual property (IP) management and protection
  • Technology readiness levels and technology transfer
  • Market assessment and business strategy
  • Developing a commercialisation roadmap for one’s own research

How learning is delivered

Industry-led workshops

Companies working in a particular field develop and deliver workshops, which can be recorded and uploaded to the platform.

CPD-accredited learning

NiCER collaborates with professional bodies and councils so that learners earn continuing professional development (CPD) points. For example, the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA) requires its members to engage in continuous learning.

SETA-accredited courses

Selected courses are accredited through the relevant Sector Education and Training Authorities (SETAs), such as BANKSETA, so that learners receive recognised accreditation.

Academic partnerships

Courses are delivered in collaboration with academic institutions, including universities and private colleges.

Formats

Live workshops, pre-recorded courses, masterclasses and cohort-based programmes.

Skills development spend for companies

Companies are required to invest a share of their payroll in skills development and many have skills development targets to meet, through both accredited and non-accredited learning. The Academy offers companies a ready catalogue of relevant courses, pre-recorded or coordinated by NiCER, to help them spend their skills development budgets meaningfully and meet their targets.

This is also a source of sustainable income for NiCER.

Featured programme: Sasol Research Capacity Building

NiCER, with iCatalysis Hub, trains a cohort of Sasol-NRF funded second-year Master’s and PhD students across seven South African universities. The output is a cohort of commercialisation-ready postgraduate talent, which feeds the Research to Innovation Lab.

Research commercialisation

Guide students towards a structured pathway to transform research into viable commercial ventures.

Capacity building

Enhance students’ skills in business strategy, market assessment and technology transfer.

Mentorship and networking

Facilitate mentorship and networking opportunities.

Innovation ecosystem navigation

Provide practical tools to navigate innovation ecosystems.

Commercialisation roadmaps

Support students in developing commercialisation roadmaps for their research.
