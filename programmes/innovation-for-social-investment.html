Innovation for Social Investment

Partners with corporate social investment programmes to deliver innovative projects in sectors such as education and disability inclusion.

Objective 5Programme 6.5
Register your interest

Objective 5: To mobilise innovation for social investment

Many companies allocate corporate social investment (CSI) and corporate social responsibility (CSR) funding every year. This programme partners with those companies to direct that funding through innovation: designing and delivering innovative, sustainable projects in social sectors such as education and support for people with disabilities, among others.

Why it matters

Companies invest significant CSI and CSR budgets every year, yet these are seldom used to build lasting, innovation-led solutions. Directing them through innovation multiplies their social return.

What we do

We partner with companies that have CSI or CSR funding to design and deliver innovative projects in social sectors such as education and support for people with disabilities. The aim is to embed innovation as a long-term capability and cultural norm in how society addresses its developmental challenges.

Intended outcomes

  • Innovative, sustainable solutions to social challenges.
  • Measurable social impact for CSI funders.
  • Innovation embedded as a norm in how society responds to its challenges.

How it works

Understand the funder’s priorities

NiCER works with the company to understand its CSI focus areas, targets and reporting needs.

Co-design the project

NiCER draws on its network of researchers, innovators and community organisations to design an innovative response to a defined social challenge.

Deliver and support

NiCER coordinates delivery with implementation partners, drawing on the Academy, the Lab and IMA where relevant.

Measure and report

Outcomes are tracked and reported to the funder, and lessons are shared through the NiCER network.

Focus sectors

Education

Access to and success in post-school opportunities, learner support and skills for the future.

Disability inclusion

Innovative solutions that improve access, participation and opportunity for people with disabilities.

Youth employment and enterprise

Packaged business opportunities and support for unemployed young people.

Community resilience

Sustainability support for community-based organisations.

Interventions

CSI Innovation Partnerships

2027 (planned)

Co-designing CSI-funded innovation projects with companies in education, disability inclusion and other social sectors.

Partner: Open to partners

National Before Varsity Program

2024 to date (annual)

Based on the published book Before Varsity, a programme that guides high school learners to access and succeed in post-school opportunities. Activities include tutoring, career coaching and applications to tertiary institutions.

Partner: Technical: Ink Matter. Sponsors: MTN (2024), Adopt-a-School (2025)

Handing-Out Start-ups

2024 to date

Packaging established start-ups or business-in-a-box opportunities (for example a shoe-cleaning pack) and helping unemployed youth to run them.

Partner: Tailor Cleanik

Sustainability for CBOs

2026

Supporting community-based organisations to achieve sustainability and resilience outcomes.

Partner: Young African Entrepreneurs Institute
