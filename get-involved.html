Ways to work with NiCER

If you are…

A company

Sponsor an industry problem in the Lab; use the Academy for your skills development spend; channel CSI funding through innovation; host workshops as an industry expert.

A university or research institution

Bring IP into the Lab; partner on accredited courses; nominate postgraduate students; join communities of practice.

A government entity

Partner on capability building for officials; co-develop innovation-led district or regional programmes.

A funder or investor

Co-invest in Lab projects and IMA ventures; fund programmes aligned to your mandate.

A professional body or SETA

Accredit Academy courses for CPD points or SETA credits.

An ecosystem organisation

Post on the NiCER Notice Board; present at communities of practice; collaborate on joint initiatives.
