How we partner

Partnerships are formalised through memoranda of understanding (MOUs), which set out roles, contributions, confidentiality and, where relevant, IP ownership and benefit-sharing, before work begins.

NiCER acts as partnership custodian: it signs MOUs with partners and holds the IP and confidentiality framework.
