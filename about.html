About NiCER

A research-anchored developmental platform that turns knowledge into innovation, commercial value and entrepreneurial outcomes.

NiCER champions innovation, commercialisation and entrepreneurship as one integrated system.

NiCER (Network for Innovation, Commercialization and Entrepreneurship anchored on Research) is a non-profit organisation established to champion the Innovation–Commercialization–Entrepreneurship (ICE) Nexus as an integrated system for advancing sustainable economic and societal development.

NiCER operates as a research-anchored developmental platform designed to enable the systematic translation of knowledge into innovation, commercial value and entrepreneurial outcomes. Rather than supporting isolated interventions, NiCER strengthens the connective tissue between research, markets and enterprise creation.

The organisation works across sectors and geographies. It engages researchers, innovators, entrepreneurs, industry, policymakers and funders within and beyond national boundaries to improve ecosystem coherence and long-term impact.

The ICE Nexus

When these elements are pursued in silos, the result is research that stays on the shelf, start-ups without technology and companies without access to new knowledge.

Innovation

Turning knowledge into new or improved products, services, processes and models that solve real problems.

Commercialisation

Taking innovations to market through company adoption, licensing or new ventures, so that they create economic and social value.

Entrepreneurship

Building enterprises, and entrepreneurial mindsets, that carry innovations forward, create jobs and sustain impact.

Research, the anchor

Applied research and knowledge creation, including publicly funded research and indigenous knowledge systems (IKS), are the foundation from which innovation grows.

NiCER is founded on the premise that sustainable development emerges when the ICE Nexus functions as a coherent, mutually reinforcing system, anchored in applied research and knowledge creation and oriented toward real-world value creation. The little resources that exist in the ecosystem must be optimised, not duplicated.

Vision

A world in which research-driven innovation is systematically transformed into sustainable economic and societal value.

Mission

To champion innovation for sustainable development in Africa, by championing the Innovation–Commercialization–Entrepreneurship (ICE) Nexus and enabling the translation of research outcomes into innovative solutions, sustainable enterprises and long-term developmental impact.

Our values

Inclusion

Opening the innovation ecosystem to students, communities, emerging entrepreneurs, people with disabilities and under-served regions, not only established players.

Network logic

Creating value through connections: linking players, sharing information and pooling resources rather than duplicating them.

Sector integration

Working across industry, academia, government, funders and civil society, and across industries, so that solutions travel.

Research anchoring

Grounding innovation in evidence, applied research and existing knowledge, including indigenous knowledge systems.

Collective leadership

Leading with partners rather than over them, and sharing credit, decisions and benefits fairly.

Outcome orientation

Measuring success by what reaches the market and society: adopted solutions, new ventures, skilled people and improved lives.

Our role in the ecosystem

NiCER is a champion of the ICE Nexus, a convener and connector, a builder of capability, a generator of insight, and a developmental intermediary between research, markets and entrepreneurship. In practice, that means six operational roles.

Innovation platform

A single entry point where industry, universities, students, government and funders meet, share information and co-invest.

Convener and matchmaker

Brings partners to one table and matches industry problems with students, research and intellectual property.

Partnership custodian

Signs memoranda of understanding (MOUs) with partners and holds the IP and confidentiality framework.

Programme manager

Runs stage gates and tracks progress of projects through the technology readiness levels (TRLs).

Ecosystem coordinator

Aligns sector commissions, incubators, accelerators and funders to avoid duplication.

Talent pipeline

Feeds participants from capability-building programmes into technology development and venture projects.

How NiCER addresses fragmentation

Fragmentation means duplication and waste, and it also means innovations that already exist never reach the people who need them. NiCER responds on two fronts: connecting and coordinating ecosystem players, and encouraging the adoption of innovation.

Duplication

One shared pipeline of problems, projects and opportunities, made visible through the NiCER Notice Board and communities of practice.

Lost opportunities

Industry needs systematically matched to research, students and existing IP.

Wastage

Pooled funding, facilities and expertise across partners.

Under-used innovation

Making proven solutions visible, connecting them with potential users, and helping to remove barriers to uptake such as awareness, affordability, finance, skills and trust.

Built around global, continental and national frameworks

Theory of change

How NiCER’s resources and activities lead, step by step, to long-term impact.

Problem

  • Fragmented innovation ecosystems
  • Poor translation of research into market outcomes
  • Limited commercialisation of publicly funded research
  • Low adoption of existing innovations
  • Underutilisation of innovation as a development mechanism

Resources

  • Innovation and commercialisation expertise
  • Applied research and analytical capacity
  • Ecosystem networks and convening power
  • Platforms and programme infrastructure
  • Governance and institutional alignment
  • Partner funding: industry sponsorship, CSI budgets, skills development funds and investors

Activities

  • Ecosystem convening and network building
  • Industry-led and accredited capability building
  • Supply- and demand-driven technology development
  • Structured acceleration of innovation to market and use
  • CSI-funded social innovation projects

Outputs

  • Functional ICE collaboration networks and a shared notice board
  • Commercialisation-ready talent and accredited learners
  • Technologies advanced along the TRLs, and validated prototypes
  • Commercialised products, services and ventures
  • Funded social innovation projects

Outcomes

  • Sustainable economic and social development
  • Innovation-led responses to systemic challenges
  • Resilient, knowledge-driven markets and societies
  • Long-term value creation from research and innovation

Impact

  • Innovation working for sustainable development in Africa

How NiCER is sustained

As a non-profit company, NiCER reinvests all income in its mission. It aims for financial sustainability through a diversified mix of income streams linked to its programmes.

Promoted listings

Organisations paying to promote posts on the NiCER Notice Board. Programme 6.1.

Learning and skills development

Course fees and companies’ skills development spend on accredited and non-accredited learning. Programme 6.2.

Industry sponsorship

Companies sponsoring industry problems, bursaries and pilots in the Lab. Programme 6.3.

Programme partnerships

Funded capacity-building programmes delivered for corporate, government and institutional partners. Programme 6.2, 6.3.

Venture participation

Participation in the success of ventures supported, under fair and equitable IP and equity arrangements. Programme 6.3, 6.4.

CSI and CSR funding

Corporate social investment directed through innovation projects. Programme 6.5.

Grants and donor funding

Development partners and foundations aligned with NiCER’s mission. Programme All.
